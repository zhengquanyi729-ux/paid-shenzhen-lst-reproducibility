## Why this file, and not a `LICENSE`

A repository that ships a file named `LICENSE` containing proposed-but-unconfirmed terms
("MIT proposed", "AUTHOR TO CONFIRM") can be mistaken for a grant of those terms. It is not. To
avoid that ambiguity, this repository ships **no `LICENSE` file** and states the status here
instead.

## What will be specified later

- **Data and derived products** — the analysis-ready tables under `data/`, the frozen split, the
  candidate registry, the validation reports and the figures.

These may carry different licences and are **not** decided here.
