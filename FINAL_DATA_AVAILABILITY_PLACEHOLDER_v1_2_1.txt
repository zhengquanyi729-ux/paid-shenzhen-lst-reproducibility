The frozen analysis-ready data and derived artefacts required to audit and reproduce the reported
analyses are available in the PAID Shenzhen LST reproducibility repository {{DATA_ARCHIVE_DOI}}.
During peer review they are accessible through {{DATA_REVIEWER_LINK}}. The release contains the
complete 32,748-cell Shenzhen spatial split (the full 233 x 362 analysis grid, EPSG:32649) and the
exact frozen analysis-ready covariate and land-surface-temperature tables used for Regions A, B and C,
together with the candidate registries, the Stage-04 evaluation artefacts, the Stage-05 benchmark
outputs, figure-source data and the protocol-audit records. The complete Region-C analysis-ready table
(n = 2,987) is provided solely so that the published locked-region statistic can be recomputed; Region

confirmatory holdout. The approximately 857-MB original Shenzhen source-raster archive is not included
in the core release because redistribution rights and source metadata are incomplete; instead the
exact frozen analysis-ready values used in the manuscript and a source-data provenance inventory are
provided. Changchun row-level analysis-ready data are distributed only to the extent permitted by the
applicable third-party data terms; where redistribution is not permitted, the archive provides the
analysis code, schema, provenance, hashes and derived outputs instead.
